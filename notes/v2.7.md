Please welcome /e/OS 2.7! :rocket:

We are proud to deliver the /e/OS 2.7. Enjoy all the new features and improvements it embeds!

## ✨ We embedded some improvements! 


:shopping_bags: **App Lounge**

- App Lounge can now update Launcher and eDrive

:clock8: **Clock**

- All toggles are now following our palette

:iphone: **Galaxy S20, S20+ and S20 Ultra** are now available with /e/OS

:iphone: **Google Pixel Tab**

- `Communal settings` have been renamed into `Dock and lock screen options` for more clarity

:love_letter: **Mail**

- It's now possible to login into a Microsoft account and manage the associated emails in Mail

🦸 **Third Party Apps support**

- microG UI is now consistent with our design guidelines

## 🐛 Bug fixes


:shield: **Advanced Privacy**

- The Fake my IP persistent notification is now visible again.

:shopping_bags: **App Lounge**

- App Lounge self update is now working for all kind of builds

:date: **Calendar** 

- About screen is now accurate

:camera: **Camera**

- The camera change button and the pause button are no longer overlapping after changing orientation in video mode.

:camera: **Fairphone Camera**

- Fairphone Camera now works smoothly in dark conditions (automatic night light)

:iphone: **CMF Phone 1**

- `Always on display` has been set to off by default to avoid battery drain

:iphone: **Fairphone 4**

- eSIM is supported again

:iphone: **Motorola Moto G7 Play** won't be built on /e/OS based on AOSP 14 because of its limited partition size.

:sunrise: **Launcher**

- Launcher is more stable and no longer crashes in emulator or when installing and launching many apps via sideload or after installation on the Pixel 6 Pro
