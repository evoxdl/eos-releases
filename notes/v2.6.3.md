## 🐛 Bug fixes

:shopping_bags: **App Lounge**

- For already purchased apps the detail page now shows `Install` instead of the price
- Anonymous login fixed after some recent unstability (the fix was also delivered with App Lounge's auto update)

:globe_with_meridians: **Browser**

- External intents are again supported in Browser

:camera: **Camera**

- Camera no longer crashes when going to Photo settings in French and Spanish

:children_crossing: **Parental Control**

-  It's now possible to rotate the screen while setting Parental Control without the app going back to the welcome screen

🦸 **Third Party Apps support**

- Google apps are working again
