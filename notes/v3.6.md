Please welcome **/e/OS 3.6**! :rocket:

We are proud to deliver the /e/OS 3.6 version for supported devices whether they are on an Official or a Community build.
Enjoy all the new features and improvements it embeds!

**This is the first /e/OS release based on AOSP 16.** :star_struck: It includes the [improvements](https://gitlab.e.foundation/e/os/releases/-/releases#-we-embedded-some-improvements) and [bug fixes](https://gitlab.e.foundation/e/os/releases/-/releases#-bug-fixes) which have been developed for AOSP 15.
