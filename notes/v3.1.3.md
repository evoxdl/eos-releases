Please welcome /e/OS 3.1.3! :rocket:

This is a stability and maintenance release for The Fairphone (Gen. 6) on Official. This release urgently addresses critical regressions introduced in the prematurely released 3.1.2 build. 

This update includes all the intended features of version 3.1.2, plus the following critical fixes:
  - Radio & Connectivity: Fixed a major regression that prevented the SIM card from registering on mobile networks.
  - Hardware Integration: Restored full functionality to the device's hardware slider button.
  - Regional Compliance: Corrected the implementation of FCC certification changes, ensuring full compatibility for operation in the United States.
