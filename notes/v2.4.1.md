Please welcome /e/OS 2.4.1! :rocket:

:warning: 2.4.1 is a re-publication of our 2.4 version which was made unavailable following the [critical outage which is happening since October, 6th](https://community.e.foundation/t/update-on-murena-io-service-outage/61781). Please **install it even if you had already installed 2.4** :pray:

Enjoy all the new features and improvements /e/OS 2.4.1 embeds!

## ✨ We embedded some improvements! 

:iphone: **Pixel 7** is now available with /e/OS as official device :tada:

:man_in_tuxedo_tone3: **Account Manager**

- Account Manager is now updatable through App Lounge as any other apps

:shopping_bags: **App Lounge**

- App Lounge can now auto update itself

:globe_with_meridians: **Browser**

- Legacy ad blocking filter is now hosted on gitlab.e.foundation as bromite.org's domain name was about to expire

:date: **Calendar** 

- `ics` files containing multiple events can now be added properly to your calendar. Return trips, here we come!

:iphone: **Fairphone 4 and 5**

- You'll now be able to enjoy Fairphone's default camera app on your Fairphone 5 to take pictures and videos if you want to, you just need to enable one setting and reboot your phone
- The video mode of Fairphone's default camera app is now working on the Fairphone 4

:camera: **Camera**

- UX when privacy switch is turned on on Murena Two is now easily understandable

:calling: **Updater**

- Channel names and builds are now called `official` (instead of `stable`) and `community` (instead of `dev`).
